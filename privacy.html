1. Introduction

ProCash Manager ("the app", "we") is a personal budget tracker and group expense splitter. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information (only if you sign in with Google): your name, email address and profile photo, provided by Google Sign-In through Firebase Authentication.
  • Financial information you enter: expense descriptions, amounts, dates, categories, your monthly income, category budget limits, and cloud backups you create.
  • Group data (only if you use Group Split): group name, member names, group expenses, and the account IDs of members. This data is visible to every member of that group.
  • Subscription information (ProCash Pro): your plan status and renewal date. Payments are processed by Razorpay (India) or our international payment partner; we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Data stored on your device: theme, currency, a SHA-256 hash of your app PIN (never the PIN itself), and a local copy of your expenses so the app works offline.

We do not collect your contacts, precise location, SMS, or photos library.

3. How We Use Your Data

Only to provide the features of the app: showing your expenses and reports, syncing your data across your devices, creating backups, and calculating group balances. We do not sell or rent your data, and the app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

4. Optional Features

  • Receipt scan: the photo is read on your device using on-device text recognition. The image is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Voice input: uses your browser's or phone's speech recognition. Depending on your device, the audio may be processed by the speech service of your device or browser provider (for example Google). We do not record or store audio.
  • PDF / CSV export: files are created on your device and saved only where you choose.

5. Third-Party Services

For paid subscriptions we use Razorpay Software Pvt Ltd (customers in India) and Lemon Squeezy (customers elsewhere), which act under their own privacy policies. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

The app uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to sign you in and store your data, operated by Google LLC under its own privacy policy. Some open-source libraries are loaded from public content delivery networks (such as jsDelivr, cdnjs and gstatic), which may receive your IP address as part of normal web requests.

6. Data Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and protected in Firebase by security rules so that each user can access only their own data and the groups they belong to. The app PIN is a convenience lock stored on your device; it is not a substitute for your device's screen lock.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • In the app: More → Reports → Delete account (visible when signed in) permanently deletes your account, your expenses, backups, and the groups you created, and removes you from other groups. Clear all data removes the data on this device and your cloud expenses.
  • By request: see how to delete your account and data. Requests sent by email are completed within 30 days.
  • Group expenses you added to a group you did not create may remain visible to that group's other members.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
