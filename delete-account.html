Delete Your Account and Data

Last Updated: October 2026

खाता और डेटा हटाने का तरीका (Hindi): ऐप खोलें → नीचे "और/Reports" पेज → अकाउंट डिलीट करें दबाएँ। आपका अकाउंट, खर्च, बैकअप और आपके बनाए ग्रुप हमेशा के लिए हट जाएँगे।

Option 1: Delete inside the app (instant)

  1. Open ProCash Manager and sign in with Google.
  2. Go to the Reports page and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete account (अकाउंट डिलीट करें) and confirm. You may be asked to sign in with Google again for security.

What is deleted: your sign-in account, all your cloud expenses, your cloud backup, and every group you created. You are also removed from groups created by others.

What may remain: expenses you added to someone else's group stay in that group so its other members keep a correct record. Data stored only on your own device is removed when you choose Clear all data or uninstall the app.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Email YOUR_EMAIL@example.com from the address you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my ProCash account". We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.
