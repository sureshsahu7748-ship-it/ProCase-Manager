Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Plans

ProCash Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription: ₹29 per month or ₹299 per year for customers in India (paid via Razorpay) and US$5 per month or US$25 per year for customers elsewhere (paid via our payment partner). Prices may include applicable taxes.

Cancel anytime

You can cancel in the app: Settings → ProCash Pro → Cancel subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for, and you will not be charged again.

Refunds

If you are not satisfied, email us within 7 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full. Renewal payments are not refundable unless you were charged after a cancellation or by mistake. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

Failed payments

If a renewal fails, Pro stays active for a short grace period and then ends. Your free features and data are not deleted.
